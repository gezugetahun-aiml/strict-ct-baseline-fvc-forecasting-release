# Reviewer 2 Comment 2 — baseline-only outer-fold sensitivity

This post-hoc sensitivity analysis addresses the concern that the submitted five outer validation folds were balanced partly with outcome-derived forward FVC slope and other future-derived fold descriptors.

## What changed

Only the **outer patient partition** was changed. The sensitivity outer-fold assignment used prediction-origin variables only:

- Age
- Sex
- SmokingStatus
- baseline FVC
- Percent-predicted FVC
- baseline week relative to CT
- CT-derived FSS density proxy
- patient-count balancing

The assignment did **not** use PatientSlope, rapid-decliner status, number of future targets, mean/max future horizon, future FVC/Delta-FVC, prediction error, or any other held-out outcome-derived quantity.

## What remained frozen

The strict CT-baseline cohort, CT preprocessing, DenseNet169 representations, inferior/whole-lung banks, all seven experts, neural training settings, seeds, rapid-decline weighting within outer-training patients, inner development split, expert fusion, uncertainty calibration, scoring, and patient-cluster bootstrap were unchanged from frozen v10.

Keeping the inner development procedure unchanged is intentional: the sensitivity isolates the reviewer's concern about **outer validation partitioning**. Outcome-derived quantities from outer-training patients remain development information and do not enter outer-validation patient predictors.

## Aggregate sensitivity result

The same 29 patients and 231 strictly post-CT targets were evaluated.

| Metric | Submitted v10 | Baseline-only outer folds | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| MAE (mL) | 169.416 | 198.402 | +28.986 |
| RMSE (mL) | 243.886 | 267.997 | +24.111 |
| Modified LLL | -6.954222 | -7.053661 | -0.099438 |
| Absolute errors >=500 mL | 14 | 18 | +4 |

Paired 5000-resample patient-cluster bootstrap comparison, seed 20260818:

- Delta MAE: +28.986 mL, 95% CI 1.306 to 57.002 mL.
- Delta RMSE: +24.111 mL, 95% CI -3.619 to 50.666 mL.
- Delta LLL: -0.099438, 95% CI -0.321323 to 0.133370.
- Delta N500: +4, 95% CI -1 to 10.

The adjusted Rand index between the submitted and sensitivity fold assignments was approximately -0.00119; only 1 of 29 patients retained the same numeric fold label. The sensitivity therefore represents a materially different outer partition rather than a relabeling of the original folds.

## Interpretation

The sensitivity analysis indicates that outer-fold composition materially affects the estimated MAE in this small cohort. The submitted partition remains the prespecified primary analysis, while the baseline-only partition is reported as a post-hoc reviewer-requested sensitivity analysis. Both estimates should be regarded as exploratory pending external validation.
